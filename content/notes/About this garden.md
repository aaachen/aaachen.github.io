I write with the express purpose of unraveling thoughts in my head

A close family friend once gave me a nickname, which in retrospect perfectly characterizes me: 问题青年. It is a play on word to mean a delinquent and a kid with lots of questions.

Being constantly troubled by questions (and an answer) is perhaps both a personal vice and virtue. Well, mostly a vice if you ask me, since this obsession is a pain to live with: I tend to over-complicate things when things are just "not that deep bro". I often lack an intuitive grasp to reality and rely more on ideas as a proxy - which is to say, I live in my head more often than getting my hands dirty. I would often feel incredibly *stuck* unless all those thoughts get cleanly connected somehow.

I started this digital garden as an attempt to answer questions that have bothered me for a long time. A digital garden is a medium between half-baked notes and blog, between messages written to self and written for broadcast. 

It is an open experiment for myself to both
1. Iterate faster on thoughts by flushing them out of my brain
2. An invitation / call to action to *make things* out of them

Most of the time, there's only one intended audience to those writings - myself in the future. A secondary benefit in sharing them is to open up for feedback from those equally interested in those topics (and more of an expert than me!). 

I believe in a human centered network as a way to amplify signals than big algos that farm attention solely for monetization

Related ethos I share:
- [Work with the garage door up](https://notes.andymatuschak.org/zCMhncA1iSE74MKKYQS5PBZ)
- [A blog post is a very long and complex search query to find fascinating people and make them route interesting stuff to your inbox](https://www.henrikkarlsson.xyz/p/search-query)
- [Learn in the public](https://www.swyx.io/learn-in-public) (with distilled thought instead of noise)
